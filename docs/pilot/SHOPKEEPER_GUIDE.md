# AutoPrint: guide for the shop

Students send a PDF from their phone. It appears on your computer. You look, you press one button, it prints. They pay you at the counter, as always.

**Problem? Phone ____________________________ (founder)**

## What you see

- **Top right:** a green dot and "Connected" means all is well. Orange and "No internet" means the internet is down; wait, it reconnects by itself.
- **Requests:** what needs you now. **Finished:** the last 24 hours. Type an order code there to find any order.
- You hear a sound when a request arrives, and again while one is waiting: three times 2 minutes apart, then every 10 minutes. The sound stops when you answer, when the request's hour is over, or while there is no internet.

Each request is one card:

| On the card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ORDER CODE** (4 characters, like K7QD) | The student says this code at the counter. Match it before you hand over pages. |
| File name, pages, copies, **Colour** or Black and white, One side or Both sides | What the student asked for. |
| "6 sides to print on 3 sheets of paper" | How many sheets should come out. Count them. |
| **TO COLLECT ₹...** | What the student pays you. |
| "Waiting 2 min · Expires in 58 min if not approved" | You have 1 hour to answer. After that it closes and the student must send again. |

## The buttons

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Approve and print** | Prints it now. If something is already printing, it waits its turn. |
| **Preview** | Shows the pages first. You can approve or reject from there. |
| **Reject** | Refuses it, with one click. Nothing prints. The student only sees "The shop declined this print", so tell them why. |
| **Settings** | Choose the printer, print a test page, turn the sound on or off. If you have a second printer for colour, choose it there too and press **Test the colour printer**. |
| **X** (close the window) | The window hides but AutoPrint keeps working. Open it again from the small printer icon near the clock. Do not use "Quit" during shop hours. |

When it has printed, the card moves to **Finished** as "Sent to printer". Check the pages are really there, take the money, hand them over.

## "Needs your attention" (red card)

AutoPrint could not be sure the pages came out. It will **never print again by itself**. Look at the printer, then choose:

1. **Fix the printer first** (paper, jam, switched off). Wait a minute. Often the pages come out now.
2. Pages all there? Press **It printed**.
3. Nothing usable came out and nothing more is coming? Press **It did not print**. Ask the student to send the file again.
4. **Print again** prints the whole document one more time. Use it only when you are sure nothing is still on its way out. If you are not sure, phone.

## Messages at the top

| Message | Do this |
|---|---|
| No internet connection | Check the Wi-Fi or cable. Requests arrive by themselves when it is back. |
| Windows says the printer is offline / out of paper / has a paper jam | Fix the printer. You can still approve. |
| No printer is chosen / the printer is not installed any more | Press **Choose printer**, pick your printer, Save. |
| "... makes a file, not paper" | The wrong printer is chosen. Press **Choose printer** and pick your real one. |
| The part of AutoPrint that sends documents to the printer is missing | Phone. |
| A code and "Connect this computer to your shop" instead of your requests | Phone. |

## What to say to students

- "Scan the sign, choose your PDF, and tell me the code on your screen."
- "PDF only. If it is a Word file or a photo, save it as a PDF first."
- "You pay here, when you collect."
- When their phone says "ask at the counter": "Give me your code, I will check."
- If you rejected it: say why (wrong file, too many pages, no colour today).

## Each day

- Morning: computer on, printer on, paper in. AutoPrint starts by itself; the dot should be green.
- Keep the computer awake and plugged in. If it sleeps, students see your shop as offline.
- Evening: nothing left under "Needs your attention".

## Phone the founder when

- The same request printed twice.
- A card says "Sent to printer" but no pages came out and none are coming.
- A red card and you are not sure which button to press.
- The dot stays orange although the internet works.
- AutoPrint will not open, or shows a code instead of your requests.
- A student asks for their file to be deleted (note their order code).
- Anything on the screen you do not understand. Phoning is never the wrong choice.

Until it is fixed, print the student's file the way you always did.
